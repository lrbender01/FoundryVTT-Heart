# Changelog

All notable changes to this **personal fork** of `hitcherland/FoundryVTT-Heart` will be documented here. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/); versioning extends upstream's scheme with a fork-identifying suffix (e.g. `0.10.5-pr94`).

**This is a personal v12 maintenance fork.** Upstream (`hitcherland/FoundryVTT-Heart`) is frozen at `0.10.4` (Sep 2024) on `main`; the maintainer pivoted to v13 development on the `new-magic` branch. This fork carries community contributions and bug fixes the upstream `main` will not receive.

See [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for fork rationale, build pipeline, and known issues.

Each has a size, turn, mirror, opacity, blend, and a hover behaviour (fade, reveal, glow, lift, or hide). They never take the pointer, and they are redrawn on every render and resize.

  - **The Art button** hides the banner and every decoration for this player (`heart.hiddenItemArt`, keyed by the party's uuid). Without the content module or the file, the sheet is unchanged.

- **Bonds, hirelings, and animals (2026-10-01, Luke; HCB p. 102, W&M W66-W67).** A bond is the relationship, not the being (Luke's call). A new **`bond` Item** on a character (at most three, enforced in `preCreateItem`; characters only; a full list refuses new bonds until one is removed, with no replacing) names who it is with and may link their actor (`system.target`, a uuid), with a kind (**person** or **companion**, following the bonded actor: `bondKindFor`; older "hireling" / "animal" read as companion), a Stress pool of 10 for person bonds, and notes; a person bond's Fallout is recorded as nested children of the bond item. Every transfer runs the GM's Fallout check on the bond (d12 against its total, Minor 1-6 / Major 7-12, never Critical); a Critical removes the bond (HCB p. 102; there is no Broken state). Companions refuse transfers and healing (W66). Each action posts a ledger card (builder moved to `common/ledger.js`). Pure rules in `bonds/rules.js`, tested (`test/bonds.test.mjs`).
